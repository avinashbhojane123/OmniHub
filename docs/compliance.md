# SafeGuard Family - Ethical, Child Privacy & Legal Compliance

## 1. Core Ethical Principles

SafeGuard Family rejects all spyware and covert surveillance architectures. The software is constructed around four pillars of ethical parental supervision:

1. **Full Transparency**: The child must always know that safety monitoring is enabled. The application icon is never hidden or disguised as a calculator, notepad, or system utility.
2. **Explicit Informed Consent**: Before telemetry begins, the child user is presented with a non-deceptive screen clearly enumerating what is recorded (screen time, locations, safety keywords) and what is **never** collected (financial credentials, unflagged personal chat transcripts).
3. **Data Minimization**: Raw conversational databases are never harvested. Only content that triggers validated risk indicators (such as suicidal ideation, sexual exploitation, predatory grooming) generates redacting safety summaries.
4. **Persistent Operating Status**: In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and Android OS standards, an ongoing system notification is maintained at all times:
   > *"SafeGuard Agent is Active — Device monitoring is visible to this user."*

## 2. Regulatory Compliance Framework

### 2.1 Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA - US)
- Verifiable parental consent is gathered prior to collecting any geolocation or persistent identifiers from minors under 13.
- Data retention is limited to the parent-selected window (default 30 days) and purged automatically.

### 2.2 General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K - EU & UK)
- **Article 8**: Lawful basis grounded in parental responsibility for child safety.
- **Right to Erasure (Art. 17)**: One-click "Delete Family Data" functionality purges all historical GPS and usage logs.
- **Data Portability (Art. 20)**: Comprehensive JSON export of all child metrics.

### 2.3 Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act - India)
- Clear parental consent verification workflow.
- Strict prohibition of targeted behavioral advertising aimed at children.

## 3. Mandatory In-App Declarations

### Parent Responsibility Notice
> *"SafeGuard Family is an ethical safety application designed for legal parents or guardians to protect minor children in their custody. It must not be deployed to track spouses, employees, or non-consenting adults."*

### Child Safety Rights Bill
1. You have the right to know what safety rules are enabled.
2. You can request temporary screen time extensions directly through the app.
3. Your private chats are not read verbatim unless flagged by the high-severity danger keyword filter.
